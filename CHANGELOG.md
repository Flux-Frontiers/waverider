  JSONs.** The opening paragraph put CIFAR-10 at 33 intrinsic dimensions,
  carried over from the stale report above; the current
  `cifar10_architecture_results.json` gives 34 (per-class max) / 29 (global
  mean). The parameter-efficiency table paired an intrinsic d of 34 with a
  noise figure of 99.1% derived from the *global* dimension 29 (98.9% at

  stated the w\*-neuron bottleneck decomposes into "d\* geometry dimensions and
  C−1 class-separation dimensions". Per Table 8 of `waverider_jmlr.pdf`, the
  geometry and class components *sum* to d\*: k₉₀ = 7 and n_extra = 9, with

  machines.** `tests/test_hld.py` and `tests/test_lfd.py` each ran an identical

  pytest`).

## [0.10.0] - 2026-08-02

### Added
- **`waverider.lfd` (`src/waverider/lfd.py`)** — Looking Glass *light-field* holographic output for any PyVista scene. Sweeps the plotter camera across a device's view cone (35° standard) with **off-axis asymmetric-frustum projections** (VTK window-centre shear, focal plane pixel-identical across views — the geometric requirement for the display to fuse them), then tiles the views into a **quilt** PNG with the `_qs<cols>x<rows>a<aspect>` filename convention Bridge/Studio auto-detect. Public API: `QuiltSpec` dataclass, `QUILT_PRESETS` (the 9 official ideal-quilt device presets — Portrait, Go, 16″/27″/32″ landscape & portrait, 65″), `render_quilt`, `render_quilt_video` (turntable + arbitrary `on_frame(i)` animation, MP4 per the quilt-video spec: H.264/HEVC + `yuv420p`), `save_quilt`, and `cast_quilt` (live display via the local Looking Glass Bridge HTTP orchestration API).
- **`waverider.hld` (`src/waverider/hld.py`)** — Looking Glass *Hololuminescent Display* (16″/27″/86″ HLD) output, a separate device family that plays ordinary 2-D video (pure white renders invisible). Renders a slow turntable orbit to the official master spec (3840×2160 16:9, HEVC, bt709, 30/60 fps) on a white background with safe-area framing and a soft contact shadow. Public API: `HLD_RESOLUTION`, `HLD_SAFE_MARGINS`, `render_hld_video` (with `on_frame(i)` hook), and `style_plotter_for_hld`.
- **`waverider-voxel-viz` holographic flags** — `--quilt <device>` (still quilt), `--quilt-video` / `--frames` / `--fps` / `--orbit` (turntable quilt MP4), `--quilt-grid CxR` and `--view-cone` (view-count / depth overrides), `--cast` (push to a connected Looking Glass via Bridge), and `--hld` / `--no-shadow` (HLD video master). `--hld` and `--quilt` are mutually exclusive (different device families).
- **`docs/waverider/lfd.md`** and **`docs/waverider/hld.md`** — CLI + API references for the two output paths, including the device-preset table, Bridge/Studio and HLD Author setup, view-count guidance, and composition tips. Both spell out the light-field-vs-HLD distinction so users render for the device they actually own.
- **`tests/test_lfd.py`** and **`tests/test_hld.py`** — cover quilt-spec geometry (view 0 = bottom-left, left-to-right / bottom-to-top tile ordering, tile-origin bounds, official preset table), off-axis view-offset symmetry and cone extent, filename convention, ffmpeg encode-arg spec (H.264 vs HEVC, `yuv420p`, odd-size padding, HLD bt709 + 30/60-fps guard), and (when PyVista/ffmpeg are present) end-to-end render of quilts, quilt videos, and HLD masters — asserting the subject stays inside the HLD safe area on a pure-white background.
- **CT / MRI demo mode** (`--ct-demo`) in `voxel_viz.py` — bypasses ManifoldModel entirely. Loads one of five PyVista built-in biomedical volumes (`brain`, `full_head`, `head_2`, `whole_body_ct_male`, `whole_body_ct_female`), extracts layered isosurfaces for skin / tissue / bone using per-dataset presets (`CT_PRESETS`), and opens an interactive viewer or encodes a 10-second HLD turntable video. New public API: `CT_PRESETS`, `render_ct_viewer`, `render_ct_hld`, `render_ct_still` (single 3840×2160 PNG, no ffmpeg). New CLI flags: `--ct-demo`, `--ct-dataset`, `--ct-isovalues`, `--zoom`, `--still`.
- **`render_hld_still()` in `waverider.hld`** — exports a single HLD-resolution PNG (3840×2160, white background, safe-area framed) without invoking ffmpeg. Useful for previews and signage systems that accept still images.
- **`zoom` parameter** on `style_plotter_for_hld()` and `render_hld_single()` — extra camera zoom applied after safe-area framing so portrait-shaped subjects (brains, whole-body CT) fill the 16:9 landscape frame. Default 1.8 for CT demo, 1.0 for manifold mode.
- **`zoom` parameter** on `render_quilt()`, `render_quilt_video()`, and `render_quilt_single()`, exposed as `--quilt-zoom` (default 1.6). Dollies the camera rather than narrowing the view angle, preserving the ~14° FOV the parallax geometry assumes while the subject fills the frame — perceived depth scales with on-screen size. Measured on the iris manifold: subject coverage 35%→85% of frame width, mean inter-extreme-view difference 15.1→51.0. The video path applies it on frame 0 only, since `render_quilt()` leaves the camera dollied and re-applying per frame would compound into a creeping zoom-in.
- **`--quilt-scalar-bar` flag** — the colour scale bar is now **off by default** for quilt output (it was always drawn). A 2-D overlay is pinned to the focal plane and reads as a flat pane cutting through the hologram; pass the flag to restore it.
- **`dark_bg` parameter** on `add_floor_shadow()` — flips the shadow gradient (centre→dark grey, rim→black) for dark-background viewers; default `False` keeps the HLD white-background ramp (centre→grey, rim→white).
- **`docs/waverider/voxel_viz.md`** — new comprehensive reference covering both manifold mode and CT/MRI demo mode: dataset tables with dimensions and modalities, isosurface preset tables, full CLI argument references for both modes, programmatic API examples, Looking Glass output pipeline, and design notes for the shadow disc and PCA projection.
- **GitHub Actions CI** (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) — lint (`ruff format --check` + `ruff check`), type-check (`ty check src/`), and test (`pytest -m "not integration"`) jobs on push/PR to `main`, following the pycode_kg/doc_kg pattern: Python 3.12, Poetry with in-project venv, cache keyed on `poetry.lock`. The test job installs `--only main,dev`, deliberately omitting the `viz` group and `kg` extras — render/ffmpeg tests skip themselves, and the quilt-geometry, view-offset, and encode-spec tests still run headless (278 passed / 12 skipped in a simulated lean install).
- **GitHub Actions release workflow** (`.github/workflows/release.yml`) — on `v*` tags, builds wheel + sdist and creates the GitHub Release from `release-notes.md`.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-05-26

### Added
- **`UniversalEmbedder` (`src/waverider/universal_embedder.py`)** — geometry-grounded, modality-agnostic dimensionality reducer with the same `fit` / `transform` / `fit_transform` surface as `sklearn.decomposition.PCA`. Discovers d\* from local manifold geometry (`ManifoldModel` under the hood) and then auto-selects a coordinate strategy via the **Manifold Linearity Index** `MLI = global_d_at_τ / d*`:
  - `MLI ≤ mli_threshold` (default 3.0) → **`"pca"`** strategy: global linear projection sized to d\* (lossless rotation, optimal for near-linear data — most tabular/image datasets).
  - `MLI > mli_threshold` → **`"turtle"`** strategy: BFS Procrustes-transported TurtleND frames + k-means++ anchors, with class-balanced anchor selection when `y` is supplied (optimal for genuinely curved manifolds — proteins, molecular conformations).
  - Explicit modes `"pca"` / `"turtle"` / `"tangent"` (raw sign-corrected PCA frames, no Procrustes transport) bypass auto-selection. All strategies output `(n, d*)` or `(n, n_components)` float32 arrays. Exposes `d_star`, `strategy`, `mli`, and a `manifold_summary` dict for downstream logging.
- **`tests/test_universal_embedder.py`** — 362 lines covering construction/repr, mode validation, shape & dtype invariants across all four modes, MLI dispatch behaviour on swiss-roll vs linear vs tabular fixtures, and the sklearn-PCA drop-in contract.
- **`CIFAR10_CLAIM_VERIFICATION.md`** — audit report verifying the README's CIFAR-10 "+8.5 pp over ResNet" claim against the raw JSON. Confirms the number is reproducible from [`resnet_manifold_architecture_results.json`](benchmarks/canonical_tests/resnet_manifold_architecture_results.json) (4 trials × 60 epochs): ManifoldResNet-UB+Drop reaches 71.83% ± 0.60% at 36,942 params vs ResNet baseline 63.26% ± 3.09% at 47,978 params (+8.57 pp at 23% fewer parameters). Includes a "What 'Matched' Means" section spelling out that the two architectures share topology, residual primitive, optimizer, and training schedule — differing only in filter width (32 → w\*=28) and added dropout=0.3.
- **`AGENT_BRIEF_CIFAR10_CLAIM.md`** — the verification request that triggered the audit; kept in-tree as a traceable provenance record alongside the report it produced.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-04-15

Initial public release of the WaveRider geometric ML stack.

### Core Components
- **`ManifoldModel`** — zero-parameter classifier operating entirely in tangent space; local PCA geometry drives both fit and predict; vectorized fit and predict via chunked BLAS GEMM for production-scale throughput
- **`TurtleND`** — N-dimensional navigational primitive with Givens-rotation orthonormal frame update; formal specification in `docs/turtlend/`
- **`ManifoldWalker`** / **`ManifoldAdamWalker`** — Riemannian gradient descent and Adam-momentum descent in tangent space
- **`ManifoldObserver`** — (N+1)-dimensional extrinsic observer hovering above the manifold surface
- **`GeodesicEncoder`** — ambient → tangent-projected geodesic distance coordinates with k-means++ anchor selection
- **`voxel_viz`** — interactive 3-D manifold anatomy tool: projects observer field onto a 3D PCA subspace, rasterizes into an N³ voxel grid (density / curvature / height / intrinsic_dim / class_vote), renders with PyVista orthogonal slice planes; headless PNG export; `waverider-voxel-viz` CLI entry point

### Benchmarks
- **Clinical Manifolds** — ManifoldModel benchmarked on six disease datasets (Alzheimer's, Breast Cancer, Dermatology, Diabetes, Heart Disease, Parkinson's); wins on Heart Disease with **zero trainable parameters** and an average **80× parameter reduction** vs. standard MLPs
- **CIFAR-10** — best manifold-informed model (4,795 parameters, PCA+MLP) achieves 48.70% vs 52.04% for a 3,676,682-parameter standard architecture — **766× parameter reduction** at 93.6% of standard performance; intrinsic d*=33
- **CIFAR-100** — Intrinsic Dim head achieves 25.29% vs 20.44% for Standard MLP (+23.8% relative, 184× parameter reduction)
- **MNIST, Iris, Digits, Tiny ImageNet** — full architecture sweep results locked in `benchmarks/canonical_tests/`
- **Protein Backbone** — Ramachandran plot rediscovery from raw (φ, ψ) angles via proteusPy; window13 achieves 0.906 accuracy, intrinsic d*=16; per-class ordering H (9.6) < E (10.0) < C (10.7) — biologically correct
